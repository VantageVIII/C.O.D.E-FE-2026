# WRO FE Monthly Log – June
(Exams & Structural Redesign)

### Academic Commitments

- Progress slowed during June due to **exam preparation and writing**, limiting available time for robotics work.

### Coding Progress – Round 1

- Continued refining the **Round 1 code**.
- Achieved **semi‑consistent first turns** during testing.
- Identified that the **gyro sensor required a 10° offset** toward the outer wall:
    - This adjustment allowed the robot to drive slightly outward.
    - Provided a wider turning range, improving reliability of the first maneuver.

### Servo Replacement – June 23

- The original **MG996R servo** burned out during testing.
- Replaced with the **DFRobot 6 kg clutch servo**
- **Reason for switch:**
    - **Longer lifespan** due to clutch mechanism preventing overload.
    - **Protects chassis** by slipping under excessive pressure instead of forcing the steering linkage.
    - **Improved accuracy and stability**, with less twitching compared to the MG996R.
- This change ensures smoother steering and reduces risk of mechanical damage.

### Mechanical Challenges

- The **original under chassis** cracked during a test run.
- Highlighted the need for improved structural strength.

### Redesign & Reinforcement

- Redesigned the **under chassis** to include **ribs for structural support**.
- New design distributes stress more evenly, prevents cracking, and increases durability during repeated test cycles.
