# WRO FE Monthly Log – August
(Regionals, Vision Upgrade & Speed Gains)

### August – Regional Competition

- Competed in the **regional round** of WRO Future Engineers.
- Achieved **2nd place overall**, demonstrating strong progress but also exposing areas for improvement.
- Round 1 code was **functional but inconsistent**, particularly struggling with navigation around **extended walls**.

### Vision System Upgrade

- Decided to replace the **colour sensor** with a **third HuskyLens AI camera** facing downward.
- This change provided:
    - **Higher accuracy** in mat colour and feature detection.
    - **Improved consistency** in navigation, especially around extended walls.
    - Enabled the robot to take the **outer wall route**, reducing collisions.

### Performance Gains

- Speed increased from **17% to 60%**, thanks to the HuskyLens upgrade.
- Practice lap times improved by **7 seconds per lap**, a major step toward competitive consistency.

### Mechanical Adaptation

- Designed and fabricated a **custom bracket** to mount all three HuskyLens cameras.
- The original two‑camera mounting method was insufficient for the tri‑camera setup.
- New bracket ensures stable alignment and optimal field of view for both forward and downward cameras.
