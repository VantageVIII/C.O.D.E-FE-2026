
# Fuse:
## Product Page Description:
Blade fuses (also called spade or plug-in fuses), with a plastic body and two prongs that fit into sockets, are mostly used in automobiles. Other common usage is in equipment with comparatively simple, low voltage DC electrical systems. These fuses are rated for 5A.

## Quick Spec

- Rated Voltage: 32V
- Rated Current: 5A
- Fuse color: Clear Orange
- Dimensions: 19 x 19 x 5mm
- Net Weight: 13g

# Fuse Holder

## Product Page Description:

Inline Blade Fuse Holders are used to protect a circuits cabling from the excessive current that could lead to damage or electrical fire. Blade Fuses are most common in vehicle wiring looms, automotive circuits, motorbikes, agricultural machinery, etc. With their simple design, they require no specialist tooling making termination easy. Suitable for maintenance, repair, and new designs.

- Max Voltage: 48V 
- Max Current Rating: 32A (Peak 40A)
- Cable Length: 30cm
