## Product Page Description:

The TTP223 Capacitive Touch Sensor Module replaces traditional mechanical buttons with modern, touch-based input. Using the TTP223 touch-pad detector IC, this compact module can detect touch through most thin non-metallic surfaces such as acrylic or plastic. It supports both momentary and latching/toggle operation modes, as well as configurable active-high or active-low outputs for flexible integration.  
  
Ideal for use in DIY electronics, home automation, security systems, industrial controls, and even medical or gaming applications, the TTP223 provides a stable and responsive touch interface. Auto-calibration ensures reliable operation over time, while low-power and fast-response modes make it suitable for battery-powered designs.

## Quick Spec

- IC: TTP223 Touch Pad Detector
- Input Voltage: 2.0V – 5.5V DC
- Standby Current: < 10µA
- Response Time: ~60ms (Fast Mode) / ~220ms (Low Power Mode @3V)
- Touch Detection Range: 0 – 10mm (depending on surface material)
- Output Modes: Momentary or Latching / Active-High or Active-Low selectable
- Sensitivity Adjustment: External capacitance (0–50pF)
- Auto Calibration: Recalibrates every 4 seconds (no touch)
- Operating Temperature: –40°C to +85°C
- Dimensions: 15mm × 11mm × 2.5mm
